Help and support

Getting started

  • Open the app and choose Learn for lessons, Practice for question sets, Mock exam for a full module, or Visual drills for lights, shapes, buoys, horn signals and plotting math.
  • On a computer, press keys 1–4 to answer and Enter for the next question.
  • For the horn-signal drill, turn your sound on. Some phones stay silent while the ringer switch is off.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved in your browser on this device only. It's lost if you clear browsing data, use a private window, or open the app in a different browser or device.

I think an answer is wrong

Please tell us the question number shown on the card (for example Q100-27) and why. Every report is checked against the current Navigation Rules and other official references.

Contact

Report a problem or suggest a fix on the project's GitHub issues page.
