What's inside

Built for the parts of the exam people struggle with

Lights, buoys and horn signals are hard to learn from a list. So the app shows them to you and plays them for you.

Night lights

Read the lights the way you'll see them

Thirteen vessel types drawn at night from ahead, abeam or astern. Name the vessel, then see why.

  • Fishing, trawling, towing, pilot, anchored, aground
  • Not under command and restricted in ability to maneuver
  • Arcs of every light, from masthead to sternlight
Sound signals

Hear the horn, then name the signal

Every maneuvering and fog signal plays at real timing: about 1 second for a short blast, 4 for a prolonged one. Tap one on the left to try it.

Buoys and day shapes

Red, right, returning, and everything after it

Lateral marks, preferred-channel junctions, safe-water and isolated-danger marks, special and regulatory marks. Plus every day shape: balls, cones, diamonds and the cylinder.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it's free, and there's no sign-up. Open the app and start.

Are these the real Coast Guard exam questions?

No. They are original practice questions written to the official module topics, and each answer has been checked against the Navigation Rules. The real exam questions aren't public.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, your progress starts fresh.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer. Turn the sound on for the horn-signal drills.

Can this replace a USCG-approved course?

No. It's a study aid. Check the National Maritime Center for the current credential requirements, and practice Q172 chart plotting on the official training chart with dividers and a plotter.
