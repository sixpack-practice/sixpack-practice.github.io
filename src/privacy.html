Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Six-Pack Practice does not collect, store or share personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies.

What stays on your device

Your study progress (which questions you've answered, mock exam scores and drill results) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. It never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Third parties

  • The pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of that request. See Google's privacy policy for how they handle it.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app is a study aid for adults preparing for a maritime license and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
